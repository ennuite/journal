This is where I intend to write about my activities in engineering.
I've gained a lot of insight over the years by reading other people's blogs, and I miss the act of writing something
other than prompts. 

At this point in my life I fear I don't have much to teach others, so this is purely for my own satisfaction and learning. A part of me
hopes that I will eventually have enough knowledge that my musings can contribute something to someone else's learning, but for now I would say that you shouldn't "like and follow/subscribe". Instead, go write your own blog!
